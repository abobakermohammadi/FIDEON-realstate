Off-market · By introduction

Private does not mean vague.

It means the right information is revealed to the right person, deliberately. FIDEON can qualify a brief before sharing exact addresses, sensitive media or private availability.

Private protocol

Discretion is a product feature.

01 · Start with context

Tell FIDEON what you are buying, selling or referring, the approximate budget, location, timing and how private the request needs to remain.

02 · Qualify without oversharing

A teaser can communicate enough to establish relevance without leaking exact addresses, owner identity or sensitive media into a public browser session.

03 · Reveal deliberately

Token-only links can expire, be revoked and be kept out of search indexing. The production backend will enforce this server-side rather than hiding content with CSS.

04 · Keep a clean trail

Access, inquiries and follow-up are recorded in the admin CRM so private opportunities are handled consistently instead of disappearing inside chat threads.
